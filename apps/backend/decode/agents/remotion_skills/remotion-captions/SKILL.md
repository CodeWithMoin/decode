name: remotion-captions
description: Add sparse timed on-screen language when a scene genuinely needs it.
---

Narration is already audible, so do not duplicate it as a transcript. Use short labels or one
brief phrase only when language is itself the teaching object. Derive visibility from frames
and supplied segment timing. Keep text inside safe margins, maintain strong contrast, and avoid
word-by-word motion unless the exact wording is the mechanism being explained.
