---
name: remotion-markup
description: Author deterministic frame-driven React, SVG, typography, and effects.
---

Read `useCurrentFrame()` and `useVideoConfig()` inside the component. Derive every changing
value with `interpolate()`, `spring()`, or arithmetic from the frame. Clamp interpolations
unless extrapolation is intentional. Use `AbsoluteFill`, ordinary React, inline styles, and
SVG. Sequence visual moments across the full duration, preserving pauses between major ideas.
Do not use CSS animation, transitions, timers, browser time, or unseeded randomness.
