## What to produce

Choose and name the teaching structure. Return its ordered sections and the ordered beats inside
them. Every beat needs a stable id, observable viewer objective, estimated duration, key points,
earlier-beat dependencies, approved-brief support, and optional example or visual opportunity.

If the planning direction contains a fixed target, estimated beat budgets must sum to it exactly.
For deep_dive, choose the estimated length this material needs and explain the choice in the
rationale.

Work from the brief. Its scope_out lines are decisions the Producer already made and the creator
already approved — do not quietly re-admit them. Its open_questions are unresolved; do not build a
beat that depends on one being answered.
